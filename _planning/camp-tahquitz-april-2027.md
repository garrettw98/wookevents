# Camp Tahquitz — April 23–26, 2027 — Event Plan

Working plan for a 3-night experimental bass / dubstep campout (Fri Apr 23 – Mon Apr 26, 2027).
Every dollar figure below is a planning estimate built from the outline; items marked **(confirm)** are assumptions I could not verify.

---

## 1. The short version

1. **This is a 250–300 ticket event.** With the artist curve you described (~$17.5K), break-even is about **225–235 paid tickets**, roughly 80% of what fits under the 350 cap.
2. **The permit-free version (≤199 on site) does not pencil with this lineup.** About 50–55 of those 199 heads are crew, production and artists, leaving ~145 paid tickets. That only breaks even if talent is held to about **$8.5K total**.
3. **"350 on site" means about 290 paid tickets**, not 350, once crew, production, artists and guests are counted.
4. **Recommended pricing:** Fri–Sun $159 / $189 / $209 / $229, Sat–Sun $129 / $149 / $169, Sunday $69 / $79. Car camping $30 per vehicle. Cabins $900 each.
5. **Book talent in two phases.** Commit Friday and Saturday's top three slots first (~$12K), and hold the Sunday closer and upgrades until sales pass 150.
6. **Late-night capacity is the main operational risk.** After midnight there are 145 indoor spots (K Lodge 105 + Dome 40) for up to 290 people, at an elevation where April nights are near freezing **(confirm site elevation and typical lows)**.
7. **Two profit shares can stack.** Kinetic Desert takes a share of profit, and the Early Bird model on wookevents.com promises up to 50% of profit. Decide whether Early Bird profit-sharing applies here before tickets go on sale.

### Rules of thumb

- Every **$1,000** of added cost is about **8 more tickets** to break even.
- Every **$10** on the average ticket price is about **$2,900** at sell-out.
- Non-talent overhead is about **$18K**. That is fixed whether 150 or 290 people come.
- If the venue bills Monday as a fourth day, add about $2,900 in cost (~23 tickets).
- The Dome at $10 per ticket costs more than the Kinetic Desert guarantee for a 40-person room. Ask for a flat fee or a cap around $2,000.

| **Day total** | | **$6,225** | **$7,175** | **$4,150** |

**Weekend total: $17,550** across 24 paid sets.

- 2:00–7:30 PM is 5.5 hours, which fits five one-hour sets. I used the first 30 minutes as an unpaid welcome slot; the alternative is one 90-minute opener.
- The top three slots each night are 85% of the spend. The daytime slots are almost free, so cutting them saves nothing.
- Sunset in late April is around 7:30 PM, so the 7:30 slot is the sunset set and lighting and projection only read from the 9:00 set on.
- A leaner fallback curve (Fri $3,650 / Sat $4,825 / Sun $3,000 = $11,475) tops out at $1,500–$2,000 headliners.

### Skewing to Friday and Saturday while holding Sunday

- Spend the two $3,000 headliners on Friday and Saturday. That is what moves the 3-day pass over the 2-day.
- Make Sunday a distinct night instead of a smaller copy: the most left-field, Stacks-style booking closes at 10:30, with a b2b or special set that only happens there.
- Offer Sunday-slot artists the first K Lodge blocks on Saturday night so they arrive early and stay.
- Add a free Sunday family brunch from the K Lodge kitchen if sales clear ~260 (about $1,200). It gives people a reason not to pack up Sunday morning.
- Allow Monday noon checkout so nobody has to drive down the mountain after the closer.

### Target artists

I could not verify current fees for Pando!, Chmura, Huck.Jorris or Ferrofluid, so slotting depends on quotes.

- Ask each for an all-in ("landed") fee that includes travel, plus lodging on site, meals and one guest.
- Whoever quotes at or near $3,000 takes a Friday or Saturday 10:30; anyone quoting above that is a decision about cutting elsewhere, not stretching the total.
- The afters are a selling point in the offer: artists get free-form late-night time in the K Lodge and Dome on top of their booked set.
- `FOLT.html` in this repo is the existing offer-letter template and can be adapted for this event.

### Booking phases

### Afters

- Open the K Lodge at 11:45 PM so the crowd leaves the Bowl in a trickle, not all at once.
- Run a door count at the K Lodge with one-in-one-out at 105. This needs a dedicated person every night.
- One afters host keeps a whiteboard of 45-minute blocks. Bowl artists get first pick, then guests, then attendees.
- Give people a warm third place outdoors (fire pit if restrictions allow, hot tea) so the overflow has somewhere to be.
- Programme the Dome as the quiet room. It sorts the crowd by mood and takes pressure off the K Lodge.
