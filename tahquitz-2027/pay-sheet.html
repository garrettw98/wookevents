Tahquitz 2027

A three-day experimental bass and dubstep campout in the mountains. One main stage at The Bowl, afters in the K Lodge and The Dome until sunrise, and a pay plan built so that every ticket sold raises what the artists, the crew and the partners take home.

The weekend

Three days of music at The Bowl from 2:00 PM to midnight, with a sunset changeover at 7:00 when the stage switches to its night look. Five one-hour daytime sets lead into three 90-minute night sets. Friday carries the biggest lineup, then Saturday, then Sunday.

What every artist gets

  • A fee that rises with ticket sales, shown in the table below.
  • The night slots are guaranteed whatever sells: headliners 75% of their full fee, the 9:00 and 7:30 slots 50%. Half of the guarantee is paid as a deposit before the name is announced.
  • A room in the K Lodge apartment on the night you play one of the top three slots, and a bed in a shared cabin if you stay longer.
  • Meals for your whole stay, and rides to and from Ontario or Palm Springs airport. You book your own flight.
  • A guest for every artist playing 7:30 or later.

Production is by Kinetic Desert. Tickets are all-in: Fri–Sun from $189, Sat–Sun from $145 and Sunday from $85.

How the money moves

  1. Fixed costs and guarantees come first.The venue, production, insurance, food, crew pay and the $9,750 of artist guarantees are owed whatever sells. Covering all of it takes 141 tickets. Below that the organiser carries the loss, and every guarantee is still paid in full.
  2. Everything above break-even is shared in fixed parts.55% lifts the artists, 24% goes into the event itself (art, lighting, drinks, hospitality, marketing and a reserve) and 21% is the organiser's. The crew get $1 each for every ticket past break-even, and the production partners earn per ticket from the start.
  3. All artists rise together.Every slot moves from its guarantee toward its full fee at the same pace, so no one is left behind while another is paid out. Daytime sets have no guarantee and are paid from break-even on. Every slot reaches its full fee at about 284 tickets.
  4. The event only grows when growing costs no one anything.Each bigger size adds costs at once: permits, a medical team, porto-potties and a second stage. So the next size is sold as one block from a waitlist, and buyers are charged only when the block is full. A step fund, held back from what is above break-even at the small size, pays part of the cost. After a step, nobody is paid less than before it, the organiser included.
  5. One settlement, in the open.Deposits go out before the announcement. Everything else is paid once, after the event, on the final ticket count, using the table below. Sales and costs are shown on the event's financials page.
