      Budgets that grow with sales

      The floor is the least the event can run on, and it counts as committed from the start. A cap of 0 means no cap. The artists' committed figure is the total of the slots you have locked on the Artists tab.

      Artist budget

        Every open slot gets its target fee scaled to the artist budget, so the offer you can make for a slot rises as tickets sell. When an artist agrees, type the fee and tick Locked. That fee is then fixed, and the open slots share what is left.

        Target fees set the shape: Friday carries the most, then Saturday, then Sunday, to pull buyers toward the longer tickets. The Bowl runs five 1-hour sets from 2:00, a sunset changeover at 7:00, then three 90-minute sets to midnight.

        Slots

        Everyone who brings production or shares the result. Each partner has a minimum guarantee, the stage it comes in at, a share of the profit, and the places it is given on site.

        Profit share at each stage

        Shares are entered in parts. Two, two and one means 40%, 40% and 20% among the partners active at that stage. A loss is split only among the partners marked as sharing one.

        Crew roles

        Everyone here is on site without a paid ticket and gets a car camping pass. Crew pay is a hard cost. Shifts needed is your estimate of the job. Coverage compares it with people times shifts each.
