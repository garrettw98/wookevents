      Payment processing

      California requires the listed ticket price to include every mandatory fee, so these fees come out of your side.

      Ticket tiers

      Tiers sell in order. The last tier of each type takes every ticket after the earlier ones sell out.

      Artist budget

      The slider scales every slot together and keeps each day's share. Friday carries the most, then Saturday, then Sunday, to pull buyers toward the longer tickets. Edit a slot to change the shape, for example when a quote comes in.

      The Bowl runs five 1-hour sets from 2:00, a sunset changeover at 7:00, then three 90-minute sets to midnight. Artists book their own flights. Their meals and airport rides are under Costs and Crew.

      Fees by slot

      Everyone who brings production or shares the result. Each partner has a minimum guarantee, the stage it comes in at, a share of the profit, and the places it is given on site.

      Profit share at each stage

      Shares are entered in parts. Two, two and one means 40%, 40% and 20% among the partners active at that stage. A loss is split only among the partners marked as sharing one.

      Crew roles

      Everyone here is on site without a paid ticket and gets a car camping pass. Shifts needed is your estimate of the job. Coverage compares it with people times shifts each.
